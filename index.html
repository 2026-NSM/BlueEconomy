About the Symposium

Where research, policy, innovation and action converge.

Vembanad Dialogues 2026 is a Global Symposium on Keralam’s Blue Economy, bringing together researchers, policymakers, government representatives, industry leaders, entrepreneurs, development agencies, students, professionals and community stakeholders to explore the opportunities and challenges shaping a sustainable blue future.

Hosted by Naipunnya School of Management, Cherthala, the two-day symposium is anchored in the emerging vision of positioning Alappuzha as India’s Blue Economy Capital. It connects research evidence with policy priorities, entrepreneurial opportunities, community needs and practical development pathways.

“Positioning Alappuzha as India’s Blue Economy Capital.”Research · Policy · Innovation · Enterprise · Sustainability · Community
Research Themes

Seven pathways into the Blue Economy.

Interdisciplinary research, case studies, policy analyses, reviews, posters and scholarly contributions are welcome.

Fisheries, Aquaculture & Coastal Livelihoods

  • Sustainable marine fishing
  • Advanced inland fisheries
  • Mariculture & seaweed cultivation
  • Coastal livelihood diversification
THEME 02

Blue Tourism, Recreation & Cultural Heritage

  • Beach & backwater tourism
  • Marine cruise & wellness tourism
  • Eco & cultural tourism
  • Maritime heritage & museums
THEME 03

Marine Industries & Blue Biotechnology

  • Seafood processing
  • Value-added marine products
  • Marine biotechnology
  • Blue pharmaceuticals
THEME 04

Climate Action, Conservation & Renewable Energy

  • Mangrove restoration
  • Wetlands & biodiversity
  • Blue carbon markets
  • Wind, wave & floating solar
THEME 05

Education, Skills, Communication & Digital Blue Economy

  • Maritime education

  • GIS & remote sensing
  • AI for fisheries & Blue Economy
THEME 06

Coastal Agriculture & Blue Cuisine

  • Integrated coastal agriculture
  • Saline-resistant farming

  • Sustainable blue cuisine
THEME 07

Blue Finance & Emerging Policy Perspectives
